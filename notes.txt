Yeh tumhari Eye Interaction template ka official spacing & typography guide hai — jo project mein already use ho raha hai, wahi follow karo. Random numbers mat daalna.

Golden rule (sab se pehle)
Sirf in values ka scale use karo: Tailwind ka 4, 6, 8, 10, 14, 16, 20, 24, 28 + project ki classes (section-padding, container-custom).

Kabhi mat karo: p-[13px], mt-[37px], text-[22px] — yeh professional look kharab karta hai.

8. Do's ✅
Har page container-custom ke andar content rakho
Section start/end par section-padding use karo
Navbar offset ke liye top content pt-28
Headings font-display, body normal sans
Max width long text par: max-w-lg (hero), max-w-2xl (section intro)
Spacing 4 ke multiples: 4, 6, 8, 14, 20
Animations scroll par data-animate, motion par framer-motion
Forms hamesha .input-base + gap-5 grid
Icons lucide: chote size={14}, medium size={16}, nav size={22}
Success/trust green: text-emerald-600, bg-emerald-50
9. Don'ts ❌
❌ Random padding: p-11, mt-[42px]
❌ 5+ font sizes ek screen par (max 3 levels: title, body, small)
❌ Pure black text text-black — use text-slate-900
❌ Dark hero backgrounds (tumhari template light hai)
❌ text-4xl jab text-display-md already defined hai
❌ Full-width text bina container-custom
❌ Alag-alag button styles — hamesha .btn variants
❌ mb-2 heading ke baad phir mb-10 paragraph — pattern follow karo (mb-6 → mb-8)
❌ Zyada colors (red/green/purple) — sirf brand blue + slate gray + emerald success
❌ Mobile par text-display-2xl bina responsive — hamesha text-display-md lg:text-display-lg jaisa breakpoint
